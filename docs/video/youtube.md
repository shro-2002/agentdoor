**Title**
AgentDoor: Rehearse your bakery's busiest day before it happens | GoDaddy Inclusive Innovation Hackathon 2026

**Description**
Other tools build you a website. AgentDoor prepares your business for its first difficult customer.

A home baker describes her shop in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Claude turns her words into business rules she approves. AgentDoor rehearses them against tricky customers and a 50-order festival rush, shows the money at stake for each gap, and enforces the rules in code: deterministic prices, signed quotes and atomic MySQL capacity, so neither people nor AI shoppers can talk the price down or oversell a day. The shop runs on GoDaddy Node.js hosting, with a storefront and an MCP endpoint for AI assistants.

Everything shown is the live product, recorded on 20 Nov 2026: a fresh business created at agentdoor.shop/launch with real Claude calls (waiting times shortened, with the real time shown on screen), and a real Claude.ai order through the AgentDoor connector. Meera's Kitchen is an illustrative bakery persona. The festival rushes are simulations; no real orders are placed by them.
